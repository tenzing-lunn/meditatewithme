### Where it touches what exists — cross-references, not a response

- **55 minutes and five to switch over** is the shape the site already has: the
  shared bell rings at :55 (`ARCHITECTURE.md` §6.3). It is the answer, in
  spirit, to question 1 of `plans/timing-message-to-jonny.md`.
- **It supersedes the 7 September "house of lighters, later on"** in
  `PRODUCT.md` §5, which now has a date (9 November 2026), a place, a headcount
  and a duration.
- **Live video is the Candle Lighter that v1 deferred.** The streaming-cost and
  safeguarding points in `PRODUCT.md` §5 stand; "no audio ever" removes the
  audio, not the identifiable people.
- **He has asked Tenzing to choose streaming software**, for the first thirty
  days and for after. That is new work outside the v1 proposal.

## Meeting, 13 September 2026 — as Tenzing relayed it

Jonny wants two options:

1. **Just the timer, and people on the map.** People meditate for however long
   they want. Their session time, their name if they want, and their location
   if they want are shared with other people on the map. Otherwise, just their
   presence: *you are meditating with X people live*.
2. **The live video.** You join at :55 and wait until the hour, with a
   notification saying someone is going to join soon.

Open questions on both are in `plans/meeting-with-jonny.md` §5.

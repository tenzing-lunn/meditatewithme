# Audit — UI/UX, 21 September 2026

Branch `dev` at `23a2165`. Judged against the four stated goals: beauty, relaxation, functionality, minimalness.
Method: five parallel read-only audits (visual system, the rail of questions, the sitting and ending, the account side, a silent browser pass at 1024×768 and 375×812), then one verifying pass that opened every cited line, settled six contradictions between auditors, and dropped anything the code did not support. Every finding below survived that check. Verdicts: CONFIRMED unless marked *partly*.

The plan that acts on this is `plans/ui-ux-fixes.md`. Findings are numbered
here (H, M, L) and referred to by those numbers there.

## Verdict in one paragraph

The bones are excellent. The earth as a front page, the two doors, the typed-out line, the candle you drag, a bell scheduled on the audio clock rather than a timer, controls that rest to nothing while you sit: these are the product, and they are right. What is wrong falls into three groups. **One real layout bug** on phones (the heading sits under the Back button on three of six question screens). **A handful of places where the product breaks its own promises**: no wake lock so a phone locks mid-sit and the sitter vanishes from the count; a copy line that says nothing is kept server-side when it is; a "finishes together" door that can leave you finishing alone; an ending that turns into a stats card with a countdown. **And a quiet erosion of the design system**: 33 type sizes where nine were declared, the display face used below its floor at a weight that is not loaded, a second set of controls inside the account panel, and a DESIGN.md that describes a switch and a button that do not exist in code.

## Strengths worth protecting (spot-checked and confirmed)

1. **Contrast is gated in the build.** `scripts/contrast.mjs:59` reads `@theme` directly. Every text pair passes AA; ink-3 on paper recomputes at 4.94:1 (an auditor's 4.4:1 reading was a mis-sample).
2. **The bell is on the audio clock**, not a JS timer (`components/Journey.tsx:300-303`, `components/audio.ts:438-455`). It survives tab throttling.
3. **Controls rest after 4 s and become non-pressable**; the waking tap falls through; focus keeps them visible so keyboard never tabs into invisible buttons (`components/Sitting.tsx:33,110-128`).
4. **Every question has a default.** A first-timer can walk Skip / Leave it out / Next / Next / Next / Strike without deciding anything (`lib/journey.ts:43-50`).
5. **Non-current rail panels are `inert` and `aria-hidden`**, so Back is instant and answers survive (`components/Rail.tsx:73-89`).
6. **The company line never invents company** and excludes your own label (`lib/company.ts:10,26-27,33-51`).
7. **The candle has a pointer-aware hint** and its whole 352×476 region is the grab target, with a proper `aria-valuemin/max` (`components/Candle.tsx:230-246`).
8. **Home hierarchy is right**: greeting, two doors, one recessive bar with *Change*, everything else behind one icon (`components/Home.tsx:138-176`).
9. **`controls.ts` is a real single source** with one focus ring per surface (`components/controls.ts:35-40,107-108`). No `!important` anywhere.
10. **Reduced motion is honoured everywhere it matters**: the lift becomes a 400 ms crossfade, the earth stills, the typed prompt appears whole (`app/globals.css:97-101,619-633`, `components/useTypedOut.ts:34,49`).
11. **Sign-in is honest** about what a guest loses; expired links are surfaced with a reason; sign-out is per device; delete is server-verified and says plainly that the local log stays (`components/useAuth.ts:50-69,305-312`, `components/Home.tsx:392-401`).
12. **Nothing scrolls on the rail.** Measured: `scrollHeight === innerHeight` on all six questions at both viewports.

## High

**H1. Heading collides with Back and the progress dots on phones.** Time, Bell and Sound at 375×812: h2 top at 20 px, RailBar bottom at 55 px. Cause: `components/Screen.tsx:140-144` gives the `middle` branch `md:justify-center` and nothing below `md`, while `components/RailBar.tsx:31` is `absolute`. Bowl is fine because it is not `middle`.
→ `pt-[clamp(3rem,9vh,5rem)] md:pt-0 md:justify-center` on the `middle` branch.

**H2. No wake lock.** Not in code, not in docs (`context/ARCHITECTURE.md:318` records only that iOS suspends audio on lock, "not solvable", which is a different problem). A phone locks within a minute of the person closing their eyes; the AudioContext suspends so the bell is late (`components/audio.ts:434-436`), and `visibilitychange` stops heartbeats (`components/usePresence.ts:164-172`), so after ~90 s the sitter disappears from everyone's count and their candle dims. The one promise of the product fails in the normal posture of meditation.
→ Request `navigator.wakeLock` in `begin()` next to `fullscreen.enter()` (`Journey.tsx:290`), release on end, re-request on visible. Behind a hook in `components/` per the portability rule. Separately, keep heartbeating while hidden *only during a sitting*.

**H3. A copy lie.** "Nothing is kept on our side until you say yes" (`components/OriginScreen.tsx:237`). For a signed-in person the origin is upserted to `profiles` on commit regardless of the share switch (`components/useProfile.ts:96-114`), and signed-in people do see this screen (`lib/journey.ts:46`).
→ Gate the push on `share`, or rewrite: "Saved on this device, and to your account if you're signed in. Shown to others only while this is on."

**H4. Name and origin are asked of every first-timer before any question about the sit**, yet by default nobody sees them (share is off; `lib/journey.ts:45`, `Journey.tsx:526`, `OriginScreen.tsx:73`). And the Name screen gives a sighted person no reason: title and lede are `sr-only`; all that is visible is "Enter your name" (`components/NameScreen.tsx:56`, `Screen.tsx:159,165`). Two personal-data screens with zero payoff, unexplained, as the first thing after the door.
→ Drop `name`/`origin` from the first-time branch of `screensFor`; ask once, after a with-others sitting, where the answer has a use. If they stay, un-`bare` the name screen or let the prompt carry the reason: "Your first name, if you'd like to be seen."

**H5. Raw Supabase error strings shown verbatim.** `signIn`/`verify` return `error.message` (`components/useAuth.ts:248,282`), so the panel can print "For security purposes, you can only request this after 52 seconds" in a product that otherwise never sounds like that.
→ Map codes to three house sentences (rate-limited, wrong or expired code, unreachable), as `readLinkError` already does for links.

**H6. The ending is a stats card with a retention nudge.** After the bell: a visible 10→0 countdown (`components/Afterwards.tsx:69-71`), "Days in a row", "Altogether N sittings" (`:54-60`), *Sit again* as the lit primary (`:91`), and for guests a sign-in control pinned to the foot (`Journey.tsx:454-463`). Streaks are the one gamification pattern the vision rejects.
→ Drop the number and the streak/total rows; keep minutes and at most "With you this hour". *Done* primary, *Sit again* quiet. Move the guest account offer to Home.

**H7. The type system has drifted.** 33 distinct sizes across `components/` against nine declared roles, including `0.84375rem`/`0.90625rem` (`components/ModeScreen.tsx:179,195`) and `11px` (`components/TimerDial.tsx:294`). The display face is used under its 1.25 rem floor on every bell and sound tile at `font-semibold` (`BellScreen.tsx:140`, `Sounds.tsx:166,194`, `SettingsDrawer.tsx:221,227,327`), but `app/layout.tsx:12` loads Comfortaa 400/700 only, so the browser synthesises the weight. Sub-floor sizes: `0.75rem`, `0.6875rem` (`SettingsDrawer.tsx:300,310,330,357`, `BellScreen.tsx:146`).
→ `--text-*` tokens in `@theme` for the nine roles and replace every `text-[…]`. Load Comfortaa 600 or use `font-bold`. Tile names in Nunito 600 at 0.9375 rem, since they are controls by DESIGN.md's own rule. 12 px floor, enforced by a test the way `caps.test.ts` enforces uppercase.

## Medium

**M1. The skip switch defaults off for everyone** (`components/useUsual.ts:15`). A returning guest re-answers time, bell and sound every visit and only meets the switch in the landing foot after a strike (`lib/journey.ts:47`, `Journey.tsx:553-557`). A signed-in member is no better off even though the Home bar already prints exactly what will happen and offers *Change* (`Home.tsx:152-175`). The second visit should be one press.
→ Default on for signed-in accounts, or after the first completed sitting; the switch stays as the way off.

**M2. The doors silently rewrite `showCount`/`untilBell`** (`ModeScreen.tsx:29-32`). Three consequences: the usual fingerprint no longer matches so skip turns itself off unannounced (`Journey.tsx:547`, `lib/journey.ts:75`, `useUsual.ts:65`); the drawer's *How long* loses its "until the bell" stop when `showCount` is false with nothing saying why (`SettingsDrawer.tsx:257-261`); and the door promises "Everyone finishes together at 12:55" (`ModeScreen.tsx:180-181`) while `doorPatch` keeps a returning with-others sitter's own length, so they finish alone.
→ Re-fingerprint with the patched prefs when `usual` is on (as the drawer does). Always offer the bell stop; choosing it sets `showCount`. Make the door's second line conditional: "Your own length, with everyone."

**M3. "N others are sitting right now" counts anyone with the page open.** Presence beats from Journey mount with a null label (`usePresence.ts:117-124,150-153`; `ModeScreen.tsx:132-135`, `TimeScreen.tsx:180-181`). Reading the landing is not sitting.
→ "N others are here right now", or use `litCount` if the word must be "sitting".

**M4. The Sound sheet shrinks the earth and cannot be dismissed properly.** `--earth-foot` jumps 14 rem→36 rem (`Sitting.tsx:171-172`); measured at 1024×768 the earth drops to 395×192 from ~790×385. No Escape, no tap-outside, no focus move on open or return on close (`Sitting.tsx:190-199`). With three beds on it is three faders, a Volume and six tiles: a small mixing desk on a meditation screen.
→ Overlay the sheet on the earth (absolute, `bottom-0`, `bg-room/90`) so the earth never moves. Escape and outside-tap close; focus the first tile on open, return to *Sound* on close.

**M5. "Until the bell" never finishes *with* the group on screen.** At `hasEnded` the stage flips to `finished` and `Sitting` unmounts (`Journey.tsx:349-370,431`): the earth and everyone's candles vanish at the exact moment the promise is fulfilled.
→ Keep `Sitting` mounted through `COOLDOWN_MS` with the clock gone and controls rested, then crossfade to the minutes.

**M6. The clock is always on**, counting every second top-right, and does not rest with the controls (`Sitting.tsx:148-154` vs `:124-126`). 3,300 visible changes in a 55-minute sit.
→ Add `${controls}` so it rests too; a tap shows it.

**M7. `aria-live="polite"` on the company line** (`Sitting.tsx:180`), which rotates names every 20 s and changes on every 15 s poll. A screen-reader user is interrupted for the whole sitting.
→ Remove `aria-live`; the `role="timer"` is already live=off.

**M8. Full-rate `requestAnimationFrame` for the whole sitting**, redrawing the base plus up to 600 sprites per frame at device DPR (`components/WorldMap.tsx:857-861`). `paused` exists (`:610`) but Sitting never passes it (`Sitting.tsx:174`). An hour at 60 fps on a phone is heat and battery.
→ Cap at 30 fps; pause while the sheet is open; on-the-minute redraws under reduced motion.

**M9. Candles may blob on phones.** *Partly*: the facts hold (13 px floor at `WorldMap.tsx:733-736`, `lighter` compositing at `:885`, a 1° cell ≈1 px at 375 px), the merging is inferred, and the mobile demo looked fine with fixture data.
→ Merge neighbouring lit cells before `place()` when the projected radius is under one cell.

**M10. Two control systems, and DESIGN.md describes neither.** `Account.tsx:72-93` carries its own field, primary, menu item and word (44 px, `ring-1`) beside `controls.ts` (48 px, `ring-2`). DESIGN.md says buttons are 2.75 rem with 1.5 rem padding; code is `min-h-12 px-7` (`controls.ts:44`). DESIGN.md says the switch is a 44×24 track, ember when on, white thumb; code is a 32×56 track inside a 44 px button, ember-soft when on, ember thumb (`Switch.tsx:40,46,61-63`).
→ Replace the Account locals with `FIELD`/`PRIMARY`/`WORD`. Then pick code or doc for button and switch, and fix the other in the same commit.

**M11. The account form half-autosaves.** The share switch commits an unsaved origin draft immediately (`Home.tsx:359`); name and origin wait for *Save* (`:317-325`).
→ Make share a draft committed by Save, as `OriginScreen.tsx:73` already does.

**M12. Metadata is stale and inconsistent.** Description: "A new session begins at the top of every hour" (`app/layout.tsx:35-36`). OG image: "A candle is lit at the top of every hour" and lowercase "Meditate with me" (`app/opengraph-image.tsx:101,113-114`) against title case in `layout.tsx:40`. `themeColor` is paper while the signed-in Home is full-bleed dusk (`layout.tsx:57`, `Home.tsx:128`).
→ One sentence for description, OG and terms: the earth, the bell at :55, everyone finishing together. One casing. `themeColor` per room or dusk.

**M13. The documents are wrong about the product.** Terms: "*Your account*, at the foot of your home page" (`app/terms/page.tsx:94-95`); it is "Account, in the menu" (privacy has it right, `privacy/page.tsx:150`). Privacy claims "no cookie beyond the one your own sign-in uses" (`privacy/page.tsx:43-44`); the session is in localStorage (`lib/supabase.ts:47`), there are no cookies.
→ Fix both lines.

**M14. `/world` is an orphan.** The route exists; nothing links to it (grep across `app/` and `components/`). It duplicates the earth that is now Home's background and the sitting's view.
→ Link it from the LiveLine on Home, or delete the route and `World.tsx`.

**M15. The dial is the hardest thing to see and the product's point.** No hint (the candle has one). The ember value in the sentence looks pressable and is not (`TimeScreen.tsx:162-168`; centre is `pointer-events-none`, `TimerDial.tsx:312`). The "bell" label is `text-[11px]` inside a 280 viewBox rendered at `max-w-60` on phones, so ≈9.4 px (`TimerDial.tsx:294`, `TimeScreen.tsx:198`).
→ The candle's one-line hint until touched. "bell" as HTML text outside the SVG at ≥12 px. Sentence value in plain ink.

**M16. The dawn/dusk toggle is pinned on paper screens** (name, origin) where toggling changes nothing visible (`Journey.tsx:386-389,493-497`; `ROOM_SCREENS` at `:79` excludes them).
→ Pin `roomToggle` only when `inRoom`.

**M17. Sign out is an unguarded row** directly under *Your practice* in the drawer (`SettingsDrawer.tsx:212-214`). *Partly*: the auditor's claim that email delivery is unreliable is not what `plans/launch-readiness.md:70-76` says (it is about sender setup).
→ A second press inline, like the delete confirm, or move it below a rule at the drawer's foot.

**M18. "Go straight to the bowl" appears twice** on the same screen: in the bar (`Home.tsx:175`) and inside the drawer the bar opens (`SettingsDrawer.tsx:197-205`).
→ Drop it from the drawer; the bar owns it.

**M19. Reduced motion lives in ten `@media` blocks** (`globals.css:97,214,268,310,333,453,460,577,619,711`) and `WorldMap.tsx:850` reads `matchMedia` itself instead of `useReducedMotion`. Coverage is complete; the "one place" claim in the docs is false.
→ One block at the end of `globals.css`; `WorldMap` takes `reduced` as a prop.

## What the audit did to the running site

- The browser pass was silent: no bell chip, no sound switch or fader, no bowl, no form submitted, no sign-out. The tab was closed as the last action, parked on the static `/terms` page (no audio graph). `about:blank` is refused by the pane so the audio-teardown rule was met by that route instead.
- Walking the doors at `127.0.0.1:3000` wrote `mwm.preferences` (`showCount`/`untilBell` false→true, then back to false/false). The verifier read `usePreferences.ts:47,58` and `lib/preferences.ts:79` and confirms the two flags are faithfully restored and the stored mix (rain 0.5, hum 0.3, master 0) was never touched. The stored JSON is now the full canonical shape, which parses identically.
- Mounting the guest origin created `mwm.anonId` and sent one unlabelled heartbeat (`usePresence.ts:56-59,117-124`), so the pane was counted on everyone's earth for up to ~60 s.
- Not seen: the Time screen's dial variant at 375 px, and the settings drawer's rendering (the frozen pane made its transitions untrustworthy; its content was read from the DOM).
- Console: the signed-in origin logged repeated CORS blocks to Supabase (`auth/v1/token`, `rest/v1/preferences`, `sittings`, `profiles`). Almost certainly the pane's sandbox rather than the app, but worth one look in a normal browser.

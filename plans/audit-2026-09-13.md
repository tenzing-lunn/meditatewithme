# Audit — the whole site, 13 September 2026

Run against `dev` at `e026a04` plus the uncommitted doc edits of the same day.
Two passes, written into one document so the punch list has one home:

- **Part A** — Vercel's Web Interface Guidelines, run as a checklist across
  every UI file. Mechanical: does the code do the thing the rule names.
- **Part B** — Impeccable `audit` + `critique`, the same method as
  `docs/design-audit.md` on 7 September, re-run six days and eleven UI
  commits later. Judgement: is the thing the right thing.

**No UI change was made by this audit.** It is a plan until its rows are
ticked, which is why it lives in `plans/` and not `docs/`.

Three repos were assessed on the way in and two were rejected; the reasoning
is in the session, not here. Short version: *taste-skill* is a marketing-page
system that bans this site's typeface and palette by name; *awesome-design-md*
is other brands' DESIGN.md files. Only the Vercel checklist was worth running,
and `DESIGN.md` at the root was written the same day by Impeccable's own
`document` command.

## Part A — Web Interface Guidelines

Source: `vercel-labs/web-interface-guidelines`, `command.md` and `AGENTS.md`,
fetched 13 September 2026. Four reviewers, one per file group, each given both
rule files in full; every MUST below was then re-read at the cited line before
it went in. Severity is the guideline's own: **MUST** where its `AGENTS.md`
says MUST or NEVER, SHOULD otherwise.

Three of the guideline's rules do not apply here and were told to the
reviewers up front: the always-dark palette, the non-scrolling frame, and
sentence-case labels (it asks for Title Case; this project decided otherwise
and the decision holds). One rule they reported anyway is void: `-webkit-tap-
highlight-color` is set to transparent by Tailwind v4's preflight, so every
finding naming it is dropped.

**Status, 22 September 2026.** Rows 1–7, 9, 10 and 16 of A1 landed with
the UI/UX plan (`docs/ui-ux-fixes.md`, commit `5ac7c4d`), re-read against
the rebuilt account panel rather than the line numbers below. Rows 8 and 18
were re-checked and found moot (Home and the documents carry safe-area
insets; the fonts are Comfortaa and Nunito now, weights trimmed in
`3851003`). Rows 11, 12, 13, 15 and 17 named `Room.tsx`, `Settings.tsx` and
`SoundMixer.tsx`, deleted on 14 September with the photographic room. Row

**[P1] Enlarging text makes the landing smaller.** `useFitToBand`
(`Room.tsx:151–210`) pins `available` to `--flame-top`, so a visitor's text

user zoom. Fix: let the band exceed `--flame-top` when `needed` at scale 1 is
taller than it, accepting type over the wax under the `open`-phase stop, or
floor the scale at `1 / zoom`. The same mechanism gives 40px controls at
1024×768 (Part A #19). → `/impeccable adapt`

### B5. Emotional journey

**Arrival** is genuinely calm: a 200ms beat, then the room and the word come
up together over 2.6s while the camera settles. Then the eye drops to
`10 minutes · singing bowl · in silence`, a spec string with middots, as the
second thing read. The invitation is followed by a receipt; the sentence that
explains the place is third. **The decision** is one tap, told in advance
what it does, which is right; but *until the bell*, the thing that makes this
not a timer, is behind *Change* → *How long* → a button, and a first-timer
never learns it exists. **The sitting** puts the one sentence that says you
are not alone at footnote size. **The ending** is still the best thing in
the product, and it now carries the one honesty slip (B3, *In the room*).
Peak-end is in good shape; the door is where the work is, and it is one
layer further in than it was on 7 September.
